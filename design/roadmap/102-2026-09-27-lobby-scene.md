## The lobby becomes a scene (2026-09-27, client + ui + art + test + i18n + docs, no engine change)

The owner's report, against a screenshot of the shipped lobby: the page looks empty, nothing on it
is more important than anything else, and the whole screen is too dark. They asked for the final
design in one step rather than an increment, and for the best result rather than the cheapest.
The design and its reasons are in [design/10](../10-ui-hud.md#the-lobby-becomes-a-scene-2026-09-27);
this entry is the work.

**Three causes, measured before anything was drawn.**

- *Too dark*: every menu screen put `Panel`'s 55% near-black scrim over one shared 384x288 hub
  painting, and the lobby added a 62% card over that.
- *Empty*: `menuLayer.ts` fits screens DOWN and never up, so on a desktop window the lobby's 280px
  column sat at its phone size in the middle of the viewport.
- *No hierarchy*: six same-height dark pills and one green fill; the 2026-09-22 divider was a 1px
  hairline between two groups of identical objects.

**Art (nine generations, seven accepted first try).** The painting, both logos, three character
portraits and the descend/PvP banners were accepted; the first CO-OP banner (a flat tan field at
luma 128) and one re-roll (blank eyes, drawn from `shell.png`, whose eye is a separate rig bone)
were rejected and kept as `_alt` files. Prompts, outcomes and the import recipe are in
`art/ui/prompts.md`. Two import findings worth keeping:

- **Opaque art ships as JPEG.** The painting is 187 kB at 1920x1080 as JPEG and would be several
  MB as PNG, in the pack the boot waits for. The banners are ~30 kB each at 768x256.
- The painting put the dais at 33%/72% where 38%/68% was asked for. The code follows the painting
  (`LobbyBackdrop.DAIS_U`/`DAIS_V`).

The `lobby` pack's guard went from 1 MB to 2 MB (`assetPacks.json`, with the reason in its
`note`): the new art adds 1.00 MB to 0.65 MB. `main`, the first download, is untouched.

**Code.** Four new files under `client/src/game/ui/`, and two rewritten:

- `LobbyBackdrop.ts` — the painting cover-cropped around the dais (through the texture's frame, so
  the sprite is exactly the viewport), an additive crystal glow on the dais, rising motes, and one
  vignette behind the column. No full-screen scrim.
- `LobbyCard.ts` — an art-backed press target. It keeps `Button`'s shape (child 0 is the box, the
  first `Text` is the label, `onTap`/`setText`/`setIcon`) because `labelFit`, `widgetOverlap` and
  `viewportFit` find press targets by that shape. Its label shrinks to fit rather than overflowing.
- `LobbyHero.ts` — the selected character's portrait hovering on the dais, with name and pools.
- `LobbyResources.ts` — one material chip per element, top-right.
- `lobbyScale.ts` — the lobby's own up-scale (to 1.5x) and `sharpenText`, which rasterises text
  in a scaled container at the size it is drawn.
- `LobbyRoutes.ts` rewritten into three tiers; `MainMenu.ts` rewritten as the shell that places
  them. `Button.setWidth` is new (the dock re-divides its row when TUTORIAL goes).

Wiring: `gameAssembly.ts` hands the lobby a `lobbyProfile` provider (selected skin + banked
materials) and adds it to the main loop's `lobbyScreens`, which is what animates it.

**Kept, deliberately**: CONTINUE and SOLO stay two buttons (the pause menu's SAVE & QUIT rule);
quick-play PLAY still takes the top slot on a portal with no save; `setAccountEntry`'s label and
the portal data notice + policy link; the maintenance banner, now pushing the header down and
re-laying the screen out when it arrives mid-visit; TUTORIAL hidden once seen.

**Not done, and why.** Weapons orbiting the hero: the weapon textures are in the `forge` pack,
which loads at the RUN phase, so on a cold boot the lobby would draw them only some of the time.
A "best floor" caption under the hero: `MetaState` records no such number.

**Found on the way.** `LobbyResources` rendered a non-number count as the text `NaN`; a corrupted
save can carry one (`accountSync.test.ts` has the string case). It now reads 0.

**Verification.** Driven live at 800x600, 1280x720 and 844x390 (the mini-game phone) in the dev
server. Client suite 7,607 tests green (322 files); client coverage 97.56% lines / 94.48%
branches; `tsc --noEmit` and the file-length gate clean. New tests: `LobbyCard`, `LobbyBackdrop`,
`LobbyHero` (+ `LobbyResources`), `lobbyScale`, and `lobbyArt.test.ts`, which decodes the shipped
files. The raw portrait fails its clamp assertions, so that test discriminates. `MainMenu.test.ts`
and `LobbyRoutes.test.ts` were rewritten for the new layout; `viewportFit`, `widgetOverlap` and
`uiSkins` were adjusted for the dock's icon-over-label buttons, the 8px card gap and JPEG.
