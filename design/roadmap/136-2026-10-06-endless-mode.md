## The Endless Descent: a fifth chapter with no last floor (2026-10-06, engine + client + art + sim + docs, no ENGINE_VERSION change)

> 能再加一章吗，作为无尽模式，给完全通关的玩家进行挑战

Asked for straight after chapter 4 ([volume 135](135-2026-10-06-blight-chapter.md)). The owner
was shown three decisions and took the recommended option of each: a cash-out after every boss,
the four chapters' floors in rotation, and a personal best rather than a leaderboard. The design
account, with the rejected options and why, is
[`04-chapters.md` "The Endless Descent"](../gameplay/04-chapters.md#the-endless-descent).

### Engine

- **`DungeonConfig.endless`**, optional: a list of finite dungeons the run descends through in
  order and then starts over. `floorCount` is one lap.
- **`world/dungeon/floorSource.ts`** (new): `floorSourceAt` (which segment, which of its floors),
  `biomeIdAt`, `isBossFloor`, `floorOffersExtract`, `floorOffersDescend`, `isEndlessDungeon`,
  `lapFloorCount`. `SpawnSystem` reads each floor's map through it, `ExtractionSystem` its portal
  buttons. A finite dungeon is its own source at the run's own floor index, so every existing call
  answers what it did.
- **`world/rooms/endless.ts`** (new): `ENDLESS_DUNGEON` over ember, frost, storm and blight, with
  its own curve (1.125 + 0.25 per floor, read at the global floor index) and material tiers capped
  at 4; `ENDLESS_ROOMS` is the four libraries in one (the piece ids carry their chapter prefix).
  `CHAPTER_ORDER` gains `endless` last, so clearing blight unlocks it.
- **`ExtractionSystem`**: offers are now two booleans, not one `isLastFloor`. On a floor offering
  both, the press that opens the portal sets `GameState.portalChoice`, and in co-op only that
  button confirms; both buttons on one tick read as EXTRACT. The card offer is rolled on any floor
  that offers DESCEND, so an endless boss floor rolls one. Once the portal is open, a press
  confirms when it includes the chosen way's button, so a press holding both still counts. That
  last rule was a fix found after the first merge, by reading the co-op ally bot: it confirms an
  open portal by pressing both buttons at once, and the first version read that as EXTRACT,
  refused it on a descending portal, and so made every endless descend in offline co-op wait out
  the full countdown for the ally. `endlessPortal.test.ts` now presses it the ally's way. `portalChoice` is cleared with the
  portal and hashed only when set, which no finite floor does. `GameState.ts` was exactly at 500
  lines; a six-line historical comment was folded to two to make room.
- **Golden:** a new `endless-descent` scenario (the extraction gate's two rooms as one endless
  segment, DESCEND only) reaches floor index 5. The ten existing hashes did not move.
- Tests: `floorSource.test.ts` (finite dungeons unchanged, the endless cycle, every segment floor
  authored and resolvable), `endlessPortal.test.ts` (both buttons, the co-op choice, the lap).
  Engine 2225 → 2249.

### Client

- **Per-floor biome.** `theme.ts floorBiomeId(state)` replaces `dungeonConfig.biomeId` in
  `RoomBuilder` (palette and tiles) and `musicDirector` (the bed), so an endless run changes
  palette and music every five floors. Every run-phase art pack already loads for every chapter,
  so nothing new is downloaded.
- **The portal popup** takes `PortalOffers` instead of `isLastFloor`. With both, Descend sits on
  top and Extract under it, and the panel grows a 48 px slot; once a press opened the portal only
  the picked button stays. `match/floorCount.ts` gains `portalOffers`, `isEndlessRun` and
  `floorProgressText`.
- **No floor total on an endless run:** the HUD chip reads "23", and the result lines are new
  keys, `results.floorLineEndless` / `fellOnFloorEndless`.
- **Meta:** `MetaState.endlessBestFloor`, migrated (bad values to 0), merged as a max on guest
  login, written by `noteFloorReached` for an endless run instead of `bestFloor`.
- **Picker:** the fifth card, `chapter_endless`, lavender frame, "Clear chapter 4 to unlock" while
  locked; unlocked, its hint is "ENDLESS", or "ENDLESS · BEST FLOOR 27" once there is a record. A
  locale sweep checks a three-digit record fits the card in all eight.
- **i18n:** `chapter.endless.{name,hint,best}` and the two result lines in all eight locales
  (zh 无尽深渊 / 无尽模式 · 最深第 {floor} 层).
- Client 8506 → 8538.

### Server

Nothing changed. `/find`, `/party/start`, the ticket, the matchmaker and `MatchRoom` all read the
engine's catalog, so `endless` is a chapter id they accept and queue separately. The extract-or-
descend choice is the engine's, so the server's authoritative run makes it the same way.

### Balance

`client/sim/endlessSim.sim.ts` (new, in `npm run test:pve-sim`). On the starter kit the careful
bot passes endless floor 1 in 1 run of 40, and nobody clears chapter 4 on the starter kit, so the
sweep carries the forge starter pair (`repeater` + `hammer`). The table and the gates are in
04-chapters. The step was chosen there: the median run ends on floor 3-4 whatever the step, and
the step decides how deep the best runs go; 0.25 lets the best few bots into lap 2.

The bot's portal now asks `portalOffers` and always descends when it may, so it measures depth
rather than cashing out at the first boss. Every finite floor answers as before.

**A stand-off the bot cannot break.** Seed 404 timed out on endless floor 2 (chapter 1's kiln at
×1.375): the bot and two ranged mobs on opposite sides of a pillar, everyone holding and firing
into it, for 400k ticks. Both mobs at full HP, the bot at 3 of 6. A player walks round; the bot's
spacing does not. It is chapter 1's room and the shared AI, so it is not endless's, but no chapter
sweep's seeds had found it. The softlock gate now tells a timeout inside a live fight (reported,
allowed) from one outside it (the door-lock shape, failed).

A god-mode run (HP pinned every tick) walks 41 floors in about 175k ticks: every floor of both
laps, every boss, both lap wraps. That is now a gate too.

### Not done

- **Nobody has played it.** Every number is a bot with the cheapest forge kit, and the browser
  check above jumped floors by console rather than fighting through them.
- **No leaderboard.** A global one needs the server to re-judge a run before it trusts the floor.
- **The pillar stand-off is still in the game** for a player who stands still. It is not a
  softlock (walking frees it), and a mob AI that repositions when its shot is blocked would be its
  own change.
- **A very long endless save resumes by replaying every tick** (a save is a seed and a command
  stream, design/gameplay/01). The god-mode run's 175k ticks replay in about 5 s headless; a real
  phone is slower.
