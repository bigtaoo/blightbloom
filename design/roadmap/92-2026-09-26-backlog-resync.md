### Five open questions, answered by the owner in the same conversation

Each is recorded where its question lives, not here: `ROADMAP` Backlog (B2, B3, B4), `05`,
`10`, `14`, `15`.

- **B2's choice half**: the shop's buff line becomes a pick-one-of-three at the line's price,
  reusing the floor-card offer. Chests stay the weapon supply.
- **B3's interior capstone**: dropped rather than deferred. The spur does the job. B3 closes
  when floors 3–4 carry a skippable variant too.
- **Co-op at launch** (`05`): both automatic matching and friends. The first is live since
  [volume 72](72-2026-09-17-coop-backfill.md), and the room code gains a co-op path.
- **Damage numbers** (`10`): a sprite atlas, generated by script from an OFL font rather than
  drawn by an image model, and tinted at runtime. The finding that goes with it is that **no
  damage number exists today**, although two comments talk about one.
- **PvP integrity** (`15`): the seed-and-consensus layer hardened the way `funny` does it, with no
  replay of either kind yet. This means:
  - a `crypto` seed instead of the `Date.now()` counter;
  - signed tickets only in production;
  - a per-seat vote over the whole result tuple instead of first-reporter-wins;
  - bounds checks;
  - checkpoint kicks carried into the settled record;
  - record, never auto-ban.
- **Juggernaut** (`14`): a 1% boss drop. Since `fd6ed6d` it was "reserved" with no route to it at
  all.
