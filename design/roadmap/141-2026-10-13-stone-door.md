## A door framed in the wall's own stone (2026-10-13, client + test + docs, no ENGINE_VERSION change)

> 按照目前的美术设定，这个门就显得很不协调了。

The report: under the current art direction, the door no longer fits. It came with a frame of an
open door standing in an ember wall. `door_locked_raw.png` and `door_open_raw.png` (2026-08-20)
were drawn for the first-generation charcoal walls. Their frame is an arch of small navy bricks
with a black cartoon outline, and the locked slab has hazard stripes across it. Volume 139 moved
every chapter to warm stone, and volume 140 regraded the faces and rebuilt them as four even
courses of ashlar, so the pillars and walls became one material. The door kept its frame, so every
doorway was a blue toy-brick arch set in a brown painted wall: the one fixture still in the old art.

### What changed

The surround is painted at load from the chapter's own wall face (`scene/doorBake.ts`). It follows
the pillar's argument and recipe from the day before (`pillarBake.ts`): a match that holds in every
chapter, with no new art to generate and no new file to keep in step with four graded faces.

- **Real masonry, not the face cut to shape.** A flat arch of three stones: two voussoirs and a
  keystone, with the joints radiating to a point below the opening. Each jamb has five ashlar
  blocks, about square at the 64 px design width, and a locked door has a sill stone under the
  fire. Each stone carries the face's paint with its joints filtered out (`capStoneColour` plus
  the grain floor, the pillar top's recipe), its own tone, a bevel lit from the walls' north-west
  key light, and mortar joints as wide and dark as the face's own courses beside it. The edge where
  stone meets the opening carries the walls' silhouette colour (`EDGE_COLOR`).
- **What the frame surrounds is kept.** A locked door's opening is the shipped fire, copied from
  `door_locked_raw.png` at the same fraction of the leaf. The hazard is the one thing on a door the
  player has to read at a glance, and `doorFx`'s flame overlay is fitted to `FLAME_BAND`, so the
  lintel and sill are the band's own top and bottom. An open door's opening is transparent, so the
  curtain of light and the recess show through as they did through the arch art's hole. The jambs
  stand just outside `OPEN_HOLE`, so a mote at the hole's edge still clears the stone.
- **One size for both states.** Both bakes use the locked art's aspect (147 x 217, doubled to
  294 x 434). `doorLeaf.doorLeafFrame` reads only the aspect, so every crop and band rule is
  unchanged. The frame also no longer changes shape when a door unlocks: the two source arts
  disagreed on their lintel by 5 px and on their jambs by 2.
- **Fallbacks to the shipped art.** The shipped elevation is still drawn when the face is
  first-generation (no `authoredTone`, which today means the PvP arena's `neutral`), when no face
  is loaded, when a locked door has no fire art to fill it with, or when the host has no 2D canvas.
  Bakes are memoised per (face, state), failures included, so one bake serves every door in a
  chapter. `RoomBuilder` keeps the room's face so `updateDoors` swaps to the baked leaf of the new
  state on unlock.

`pillarBake.ts` now exports `readSwatch`, `sample` and the new `pixelsToTexture`, so both bakes
share one swatch reader and one canvas path.

### How it was checked

An offline bake of all four chapters in both states came first, as a contact sheet. The joints
were then thickened and darkened until the mortar matched the face beside it. Live, on ember floor
1, the fixtures carry `stone-door:<face>:open` and `stone-door:<face>:<locked uid>` leaves. A
locked door shows the fire panel inside stone jambs with the joints in line with the wall below,
and an open door shows the curtain through the same frame. The kerb doors (128 wide) show the
jambs' lower blocks at twice the size, as the crop rule says.

`doorBake.test.ts` (13 tests):

- the bake keeps the locked art's aspect;
- the opening is the fire band and clears the open hole;
- over 3000 sample points, the stones tile the frame exactly once and leave the opening empty, in
  both states;
- the keystone is a flat arch rather than a slot;
- the stone is the face's colour, with dark joints and a silhouette line darker than a joint;
- an open door is transparent in the middle, a locked one holds the fire, and an open door ignores
  any panel;
- every fallback, the memo, and a lost canvas behave as described above.

16 mutants (the size, the plan, block count, joint shade, keystone focus, the sill, the edge line,
the panel gate, alpha, the tone gate, the cache key and the memo, the fallback, the lift), 16
killed. The first run left two survivors, the edge line and the panel gate, and the two assertions
they exposed were added.

### Left as it is

The open door's gold curtain (`door_curtain_raw.png`) is unchanged. It is additive light, not
stone, and design/13 records it as a deliberate exception to the palette.
