## The bot walks round what its body hits, and fights what its gun points at (2026-09-29, arena + test + tools + docs)

Volume 115 put the seats on their spawns. The capacity sim (the next volume) then showed that in
a 2-seat match, of ~835 hits, 4 were a player hitting a player. The seats barely fought. Each
match was decided by the mobs and the zone. Three things in the shipped `PvpBotController` were
behind that, and a fourth came from the map.

### What was wrong

- **It fought only seats.** The engine turns every player to its nearest hostile, mob or seat
  (`ApplyInputSystem`, `nearestHostile`). The bot decided whether to fire from the opponent it
  was chasing, which was often a different body. With an opponent out of range it held fire
  while the mobs round it killed it.
- **Its movement never knew what a body hits.** It walked in straight lines to a door centre, a
  room centre or an opponent. A pillar or wall on that line held a seat against it for the rest
  of the match.
- **Its tests used a shot's size for a body.** A bullet is a small circle. A player is 1000 fp
  across, and a free-standing wall's north edge reaches 23/32 grid further for bodies only
  (`WALL_NORTH_BRIM`, `blockingRect`). Points and lines the bot judged free were ones its
  body could not reach.
- **The map has pockets no body can enter.** An audit of the whole arena on half-grid cells
  found five sealed pockets. Two exist only because the brim closes a 1-grid corridor, and mobs
  spawn inside them. A bot that walked at a mob in a pocket was pinned to the block outside it.

### The fixes (client only, no engine change)

- **What it fights.** Fire is decided on what the gun points at: `nearestHostile`, in range,
  and a clear line (`lineOfFireClear`). Movement goes after a mob in its own room before an
  opponent further off, and with no opponent standing. An opponent with a clear shot still comes
  first. A mob is fought only if the bot can shoot it from where it stands, or walk to it
  (`reachable`). Mobs are checked nearest first, and the path test stops at the first that
  passes.
- **`ai/steer.ts`** (new): `steer(s, me, goals)` walks a body to the nearest goal.
  - A straight walkable line is taken as is.
  - Otherwise it runs an A* search over half-grid cells centred on half-grid points, so a
    1-grid gap is passable. The clearance is `BODY_CLEAR_FP` 490 against a body radius of 500.
    Walls count their brim, and the search does not cut corners.
  - It aims at the furthest cell, of the first 8 on the path, that it can walk straight to.
  - When no goal can be reached, it walks to the reachable cell nearest one and holds there, on
    a tie as well. A shot may get in where a body cannot. This cleared the one timeout the
    210-match sweep still had: an 8-seat match whose last safe room is split, the two halves
    joined only through rooms the zone had closed.
  - The grid, its connected regions and a memo of 4,096 paths are cached per map geometry, in
    a `WeakMap` keyed by the shared arena catalog object. `reachable` is a region lookup.
- **`ai/lineOfFire.ts`**: the line test is now an exact segment-versus-circle and
  segment-versus-rect distance, not samples along the line. With `body` set it counts the brim;
  `pointClear` does too.
- **`ai/roomRoute.ts`**: `walkIntoRoom` steers to the passage. It tries the centre first, then
  points outward across its width. Once inside the passage it goes straight on to 1 grid past
  the far side, rather than back to the centre line. `zoneRetreat.ts` uses the same walk.
- **Sidestep and hold.** When the line is blocked, a sidestep point now has to be free for a
  body, reachable in a straight line, and have a clear shot. Otherwise the bot steers at the
  target. It no longer circles at close range, which had pushed a seat into a pillar all match.

### The control, again

Volume 115's control pinned the spawn shuffle and read 17 distinct. That still let the skin
shuffle vary, and with the bot fighting it read 23, above `MIN_DISTINCT` 20. The control is now
the plain case the gate exists for: **one seed run 30 times, with no start delay, has to read as
exactly 1 match.** That is also a determinism check. `runMatch`'s `pinStartsTo` parameter is
gone.

### What it costs

The first working version cost 1,231 µs of bot per tick at 8 seats, against ~145 µs for the
engine. It was profiled with `node:inspector`, since the CLI profiler flags did not work under
vitest. Six changes brought it to ~169 µs: nearest-first mob checks, a dense cell grid with
precomputed regions, the 8-cell lookahead cap, the exact segment tests, A* in place of a flood,
and the path memo. The sweep takes ~80 s (240 matches, the control included).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| volume 115 | 28 / 29 / 29 / 30 / 30 / 30 | 64 / 68 / 48 | 0 / 0 | 17 |
| **now** | **30 / 30 / 30 / 30 / 30 / 30** | **49 / 68 / 59** | **4 / 0** | **1** |

In a 10-match probe, 58–65% of the hits on players came from players, against 4 of ~835 before. The average
match runs 1,925 ticks at 2 seats and 2,426–3,566 at 3–8, with the zone reaching stage 9–12.

The win split moved because the seats now fight each other. Juggernaut is no longer last, and
skirmisher is, at 27%. Four matches ended in a tie, under the 5% limit. Neither is tuned here:
this sim is a first signal, not a verdict (design/15).

`voiceDemand` re-run at cap 16: its gates still hold. 4-seat PvP now peaks at 16 voices
uncapped, with nothing lost at 16.

### What this supersedes

### Tests

- `pvpBotSteer.test.ts` has 9 cases:
  - `pointClear` counts the brim for a body only.
  - `steer` goes straight when clear and round a bar when not, and gets a body round the bar end
    to end, where walking straight stays pinned.
  - It passes a one-grid slot off the straight line.
  - With a sealed box, it walks up to the box and holds, and `reachable` says no.
  - `walkIntoRoom` walks round a pillar on the passage axis, and straight through once inside.
  - The bot fires at a mob with every opponent out of range, and keeps fighting mobs once no
    opponent is standing.
  - It leaves a sealed mob and goes on toward the opponent.
- The `zoneRetreat.test.ts` gate case now expects the straight-through walk.
- Mutations: 9 killed. Seven are bot rules: aim-based fire, mob fight, the reach filter, the
  brim, quarter-grid cells, no path, passage centre only. Two are the partial path: no path
  when nothing is reachable, and drifting on a tie.
