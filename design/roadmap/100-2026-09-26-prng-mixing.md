## The PRNG hashes its seed and its output (2026-09-26, engine + test + docs, `ENGINE_VERSION` 78)

Found while measuring juggernaut's 1% boss drop (volume 97): over seeds 1..6000 it read 0.35%,
and the test had to spread its seeds to read the real rate. Fixing it turned up two defects in
`engine/math/prng.ts`, both in the bare LCG ported from `funny`:

- **Low bits with tiny periods.** `nextInt(max)` reduced the raw state mod `max`. An LCG's low k
  bits cycle with period 2^k, so `nextInt(2)` strictly alternated 0101… and `nextInt(4)` cycled
  with period 4.
- **Correlated neighbouring seeds.** For seeds i and i+1 the first raw values differ by the
  multiplier, so the difference of their first `nextInt(1000)` was the same residue 19993 times
  in 20000. `GameState`'s `seed ^ constant` stream derivation kept it (18744 of 20000). The bias
  is local to a window of seeds, which is why it showed at 1..6000 and averaged out at 1..20000.

Together they starved `shuffle`. Within one stream, however long, Fisher–Yates reached 3 of the
6 orders of three items, 12 of 24 for four and 15 of 120 for five. Every engine shuffle, room
generation among them, drew from that fraction.

### The fix

The state is still the full-period LCG and `peek()` still exposes it. The seed is hashed on
construction and every output is hashed before it is reduced, both with MurmurHash3's 32-bit
finalizer (`mix32`, integer-only). A 200 000-seed sweep over four seed families (consecutive,
two stream XORs, stride 1000), with 16 z-scores per family, found nothing beyond noise: the worst
|z| was 2.6 of 64. Other finalizer pairings measured the same, so the one hash does both jobs.

### Tests

`engine/math/prng.test.ts` gained 15 tests. Eleven fail on the bare LCG:

- a pinned stream;
- coin-flip repeats;
- no period of 4;
- unrelated first draws for neighbouring seeds, bare and XORed;
- a 1% roll holding its rate over seeds 1..6000, bare and XORed;
- all n! shuffle orders from one stream, evenly, for n = 3, 4, 5;
- all 24 orders of a run's first shuffle across seeds.

The rest guard `mix32` itself (0 fixed, injective, avalanche ≈ 16 bits) and the
`weightedIndex` walk.

`characterDrop.test.ts` went back to consecutive seeds 1..6000. The new stream also moved one
test that had been passing by luck. `rangedCatalog.test.ts` checked the muzzle distance against
a flat tolerance of 2 fp, which was never a bound. The trig table's error scaled by the offset,
plus `mulFp`'s truncation, allows about 3.4 fp at a 935 offset, and a cinderscatter pellet
jittered onto a heading 2.01 off. The tolerance is now derived from that error.

Every seeded run diverges from its first draw, so replays from v77 and earlier are refused.
Golden fixture regenerated. No balance gate moved (`test:sims` green). The client's cosmetic
LCG in `fx/filters/shieldScales.ts` reads the high bits and is untouched.
