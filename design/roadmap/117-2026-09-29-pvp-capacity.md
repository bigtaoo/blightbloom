## PvP energy capacity, measured with a bot that loots, swaps and parries (2026-09-29, tools + test + docs, no game code change)

design/03 left one question open: **does a PvP seat's energy bar ever run dry?** The shipped
arena bot could not answer it, because it never leaves the landing blaster, never swaps and
never parries. Volumes 115 and 116 were found on the way here. The first capacity matches were
too short and too alike, which exposed the stacked seats; then the seats did not fight, which
exposed the bot.

### The instrument

`client/sim/pvpCapacity.sim.ts` (`npm run test:pvp-capacity`, in `test:sims`, ~5 min). It
plays the same 90 arena matches per condition: 2, 4 and 6 seats × 30 seeds, skins shuffled
per seed as in `pvpBalanceSim`.

`client/sim/pvp/ArenaBotController.ts` is a sim-only bot. It wraps the shipped
`PvpBotController` and adds three behaviours, each behind its own flag:

- **`loots`**: walks to an unopened crate, or to a floor gun worth more than the one held (the
  PvE bot's `gunWorth`), and takes it.
- **`meleeWhenDry`**: holsters a gun it cannot pay for and fights with the blade until
  `REARM_SHOTS` = 3 pulls are affordable again.
- **`parries`**: draws a ready blade and swings when a hostile bullet will cross its reach
  within 4 ticks.

Like the shipped bot since volume 116, it reacts to what its gun points at (`nearestHostile`,
mobs included) and walks with `ai/steer.ts`. It is not the shipped bot. It keeps two fields of
memory, and whether any of this belongs in real seats is a separate decision.

`client/sim/pvp/arenaMatch.ts` runs a match and counts, per seat:

- `dryTicks`: holding a gun it cannot pay for;
- `starvedTicks`: the carried gun is unaffordable, whatever is in hand;
- the lowest the bar went, as a share of the pool;
- guns looted, swaps, parries and shots.

A per-seat start delay makes each seed a different match.

### What it reads

| condition | dry ticks | bar min p50 / p10 | seats under 1/10 | parries | wins s / v / j / tie |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipped bot | 0.83% | 0.97 / 0.77 | 14 / 360 | 0 | 34 / 30 / 24 / 2 |
| loots | **9.01%** | 0.73 / **0.00** | **92** / 360 | 0 | 31 / 37 / 21 / 1 |
| loots + blade when dry | 0.07% | 0.75 / 0.09 | 42 / 360 | 21 | 32 / 35 / 22 / 1 |
| parries | 0.17% | 0.97 / 0.87 | 5 / 360 | 9,898 | 34 / 33 / 21 / 2 |

What follows:

- **The landing kit is sustainable.** The shipped bot's bar rarely goes below three quarters.
- **A looted gun empties bars, if nothing else is carried.** It does so in 9% of live ticks,
  and a quarter of the seats go under a tenth.
- **The blade fallback erases that.** With the blade when dry, dry ticks fall to under 0.1%.
  This is the arena's version of volume 111's PvE finding: melee is the free fallback.
- **The roster's own pools decide nothing in PvP.** Compare the shipped pools
  (130 / 100 / 70) with everyone at 100:
  - dry ticks 0.05% vs 0.03%;
  - emptied seats 20 vs 19;
  - the win split within two matches of 90 per character.

  Capacity is texture here, as it is in PvE (design/03).
- **Below the roster it shows.** At 30 the median seat's bar bottoms out at half, emptied seats
  more than double, and vanguard's lead goes. That is the control that the measurement can
  move; no shipped pool is under 70.
- **Vanguard gains most from using the kit**: 30 wins with the shipped bot, 41 with all three
  behaviours. It is a first signal about the characters, not a tuning verdict.

### Found on the way: a frame-perfect parry beats any gun

One match per parrying condition runs to the 20,000-tick limit. Traced in the all-three
condition, it is seed 3,000,149: 2 seats, 3.8 grid apart in the final room. From tick ~2,000 each bot parries every
bullet the other fires, and neither loses a point of HP for the next 18,000 ticks. A deflected
rival bullet flies back toward the nearest enemy, the shooter, who parries it in turn.

The bot parries with perfect timing and a 4-tick lookahead, which no player has. So this does
not show that real duels stall. It does show that nothing in the rules ends a duel between two
parriers, and that `PVP_DEFLECT_DAMAGE_PERMILLE` (500) never gets to matter, because the
returned bullet is parried too. The gate allows at most 2% timeouts for the parrying profiles,
and prints them.

### What the sim gates

The gates check the instrument, not balance:

- The dry counter moves. With looting, dry ticks exceed 5× the shipped bot's, and emptied
  seats exceed 2× its count.
- The full bot really loots, swaps and parries. The shipped bot does none of them.
- A pool of 30 empties more seats than the roster's pools.
- The blade fallback keeps dry ticks under 1%.
- Every match without a parry ends. Parrying profiles may run at most 2% of their matches to
  the tick limit.

The win split is printed, not asserted.

### Tests

- `ArenaBotController.test.ts` and `arenaMatch.test.ts`: 22 cases, covering:
  - the loot target;
  - `bulletIncoming`;
  - the dry rule's hysteresis;
  - a blade drawn for a parry going back;
  - determinism, and a different match per seed;
  - every counter against a condition that pins it. For example, a 1-point bar is starved on
    every live tick, and only the blade-drawing bot stops holding it dry.
- The starved-seat case failed after volume 116 moved the seats apart: its dry rule waited for
  an opponent in range, and none came. The rule now reacts to what the gun points at, as the
  shipped bot's fire does. A new case pins it: a mob in reach draws the blade, and a mob far
  off does not. The case fails, as does the starved-seat one, with the rule back on
  opponents only.

### Still open

- **The parry stalemate** is a property of the rules, reached only by a perfect parrier.
  Whether a human can come close is a playtest question. If they can, the fix belongs in the
  deflect rules (a parried bullet that cannot be parried back, or decays), not in the bot.
- The bot's parry timing is ideal, so the parry share it measures is an upper bound.
- Squadmates still start in separate districts (volume 115), and the arena's unreachable
  pockets stay open (volume 116). **Nothing spawns in them since [volume 118](118-2026-09-30-arena-body-reach.md), and they are stone since `ENGINE_VERSION` 81. Open floor since 82: the chevron kit that sealed them was fixed.**
