## Every seed is its own match (2026-09-29, arena + tools + test + docs, no game code change)

`client/sim/pvpBalanceSim.sim.ts` reports character win rates over 180 bot matches: 2, 3, 4, 5,
6 and 8 seats, 30 seeds each. Those 180 were not 180 matches. The arena is one fixed map and
`PvpBotController` is a pure function of state, so a seed only changes the match through what
it seeds: the skin shuffle, and the few rolls a match makes. Where those rolls do not diverge
two games, the two seeds play the same game. This was found while building the arena capacity
sim, which runs the same bot on the same map.

### What it was

Fingerprint a match by its duration and its elimination order. Two matches with the same
fingerprint are the same match. Before this pass, 30 seeds gave:

| seats | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| distinct matches | 12 | 16 | 22 | 21 | 23 | 9 |

So the 8-seat row counted about nine games three times each, and the win-rate report weighed
them by how often they repeated.

### The fix

A per-seat start delay, sim-only. Each seat stands idle for 0 to `MAX_START_DELAY` ticks at the
drop, drawn from its own Prng stream (`seed ^ 0x0de1a7ed`). No gameplay system reads that
stream, which is the same isolation rule the skin shuffle uses. It is also closer to real play:
no one moves on the first tick. The shipped bot is untouched, since it fills real seats
(`server/src/BotClient.ts`).

The cap was chosen from a sweep of the same 180 seeds:

| max delay (ticks) | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | wins s / v / j |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (before) | 12 | 16 | 22 | 21 | 23 | 9 | 73 / 59 / 48 |
| 15 | 20 | 29 | 29 | 29 | 29 | 25 | 69 / 60 / 50 (+1 tie) |
| **45** | **23** | **29** | **28** | **30** | **30** | **29** | **70 / 64 / 46** |
| 90 | 22 | 29 | 30 | 29 | 29 | 28 | 71 / 63 / 46 |

45 ticks (1.5 s) is where every count from 3 seats up reaches 28–30. Doubling the delay buys
nothing more. Two seats stop near 23, and that is not a fault of the fix. With two seats only
the relative offset and two seatings vary, so a few seeds still land on the same game.

**The win split barely moves** (skirmisher 73→70, vanguard 59→64, juggernaut 48→46). The
repeats had not been hiding a different balance picture; they were only overweighting some
games. Volume 99's conclusions stand as written.

### The gate

The sim now asserts at least `MIN_DISTINCT` = 20 distinct matches of 30 for every seat count.
It also runs a control, the 2-seat row with the delays off, and asserts that the control falls
**below** the bar. The control measures 12, so the gate is shown to fail on the defect it
exists for. The report prints the distinct counts and the control beside the win rates.

Adding the 30 control matches makes the sweep 210 matches and about 15 s. The per-test
timeout goes from 30 s to 60 s. `README.md` and `design/15` had quoted "~6s", which was
already stale before this pass.
