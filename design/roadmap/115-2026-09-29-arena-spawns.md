## Seats start apart, and point-blank shots land (2026-09-29, engine + arena + audio + test + tools + docs)

The arena capacity sim (the next volume) runs the PvP bot on `arena_launch` and counts how often
a seat's energy bar runs dry. Its first matches were too short and too alike to be real fights.
Printing where the seats stood on tick 1 explained both: every seat of every PvP match stood on
the same point.

### What was wrong

**No seat was ever given a start.** `arena_launch` authors 8 spawns, one per district, and
design/15 says spawns are "system-assigned per match". `buildPvpEngineConfig` never assigned
them. A player config with no `start` defaults to `worldW/2, worldH/2`, which on this map is grid
(12.5, 12.5), inside the first room. So the whole lobby dropped onto one point, in the real game
as well as in every sim. The server and the client both build the config through
`buildPvpEngineConfig`, so this was live.

**Two bodies standing on each other could not shoot each other.** A bullet is born
`muzzleOffset` ahead of the shooter (1 grid for most guns) and is first hit-tested after step 5
has moved it once more. A hostile body closer than that is behind the muzzle. Stacked seats
emptied their bars into the air; in the balance sim two bots did that for 16,000 ticks.

**The bot had no idea of walls or of its own bullets.** With seats finally apart, three more
stalls appeared, each a match that ran to the 20,000-tick limit:

- It fired at an opponent behind a pillar or a wall, forever.
- It walked straight at an opponent in another room, into the wall between them.
- Two bots with the same gun, standing still at their preferred spacing, fired bullets that met
  in the middle and cancelled (step 7 resolves a bullet clash before any body hit). Neither
  ever took damage.

### The fixes

- **Spawns.** `assignArenaStarts(arena, seed, seats)` in `client/src/game/match/pvpConfig.ts`
  shuffles the authored spawns with `Prng(seed ^ SEED_SPAWN)`, its own stream so no gameplay
  roll moves, and gives seat `i` spawn `i` of the shuffle. It refuses a lobby larger than the
  map's spawn list. Client-side only, no engine change. Replays embed their full config, so old
  replays are unaffected; but the client and the server must deploy together, or a client
  would predict a match the server does not play.
- **Muzzle, `ENGINE_VERSION` 79.** `engine/systems/muzzle.ts` pulls the spawn point back when
  a hostile body's hit circle crosses the aim ray before the point the bullet would first be
  tested at, so step 5 carries the bullet onto the body and step 7 resolves an ordinary hit. A
  beam starts at the body; an orbit is exempt. Only the arena golden scenario moved (its two
  seats now start 3 grid apart in spawn 0's room). Live PvE changes too: a melee mob that
  reaches the player is now shot where it stands. `WeaponFireSystem` now asks the per-tick
  target cache at step 3, so that cache also invalidates when the player or enemy count
  changes (a stale cache failed `rangedCatalog`, with the golden unchanged).
- **The bot** (`PvpBotController`, shipped: it fills real seats):
  - `ai/lineOfFire.ts`: it fires only when a bullet-sized circle can travel the line to its
    target without meeting a solid.
  - When the line is blocked, it sidesteps 2–4 grid across it. The candidate sides are taken in
    world order (north, then west), so two bots facing each other round a pillar do not choose
    mirror sides and circle it in lockstep.
  - `ai/roomRoute.ts`: an opponent in another room is reached by BFS over the door graph (door
    centre, then room centre), skipping unsafe rooms. `zoneRetreat.ts` now uses the same routing.
  - While holding its spacing and firing, it strafes across the line, flipping every 45 ticks,
    phased by seat, and it will not strafe into a solid. A strafing bot's bullets stop meeting
    the other bot's bullets.

### What the numbers are now

`pvpBalanceSim` (180 matches plus the 30-match control):

| | distinct matches (2/3/4/5/6/8 seats) | wins skirmisher / vanguard / juggernaut | ties / timeouts |
|---|---|---|---|
| volume 114, stacked seats | 23 / 29 / 28 / 30 / 30 / 29 | 70 / 64 / 46 | 0 / 0 |
| **now** | **28 / 29 / 29 / 30 / 30 / 30** | **64 / 68 / 48** | **0 / 0** |

The win split is the only balance signal that changed, and not by much. Vanguard now leads and
juggernaut stays last, at 27%.

**The control had to change.** Volume 114's control was the 2-seat row with the start delays
off. It read 12 distinct because the seats started stacked. With real spawns the seatings alone
diverge the matches, and the control read 26–29, above the bar it has to fall below. The new
control also pins the spawn shuffle to one seed (`runMatch(..., maxDelay 0, pinStartsTo)`),
which leaves only the skin shuffle to vary: it reads 17, under `MIN_DISTINCT` = 20.

**The sweep is ~8× slower**, ~2 min rather than ~15 s. Profiling put the bot at 24 µs per tick
and the engine at 165 µs: seats spread over the map activate more rooms, and a fight that
used to end at once now lasts. The cost is real play's, not overhead. The bot got an adjacency
cache and a range gate on its line-of-fire test; the per-test timeout went to 300 s.

### The voice cap, 12 → 16

Volume 113 measured voice demand over PvP matches with stacked seats, where most shots missed.
Re-run with real fights, 8-seat PvP asks for 12–14 voices at p99 and 17 at worst. At the
shipped 12, 2-seat PvP lost 0.39% of its voices (7 of them `impact`) and 8-seat PvP 0.93% (55
`impact`). 14 still lost 6 `impact`s at 8 seats and 15 lost 1. **16 is the smallest cap that
costs no cue from `impact` up**, so `CueMixer.DEFAULT_CAP` is 16. The gates are unchanged (at
most 0.1% lost, nothing from `impact` up); at 16, 8-seat PvP loses one `muzzle` of ~8,500 cues.
What a low-end phone can afford is still unmeasured (design/11).

### What this supersedes

Every PvP number measured before this volume came from stacked seats:

- volume 99's win rates and zone share;
- volume 113's PvP voice rows and its cap of 12;
- volume 114's distinct counts and control.

Their conclusions (the bot's zone retreat, `PVP_SCALE_FACTOR` 5, the deconfound itself) still
hold. Their numbers do not.

### Tests

- `pvpConfig.test.ts`: 4 cases for `assignArenaStarts` (distinct authored spawns, the engine
  places each seat there, the seed varies the mapping, a lobby too big is refused).
- `engine/systems/muzzle.test.ts`: 8 cases, each rule pinned on the side that must not move
  the muzzle as well as the side that must, plus two stacked seats that damage each other end
  to end.
- `pvpBotSight.test.ts`: 7 cases, including the clash stall and the pillar stall end to end
  (counting `hit` events, since the shield regenerates light damage away), and the BFS route.
- Mutations: the spawn assignment, the muzzle rule and three bot rules each killed.
- `gameReplaySave.test.ts` counted every toast of a 120-frame run; v79 kills a mob in it, and
  the pickup toast broke the count. It now counts only toasts after the save.
