## The lobby's material counts become a FORGE badge (2026-09-28, client + ui + test + docs, no engine change)

The owner circled the five material counts beside SETTINGS on the lobby (volume 102) and asked:
move them to the top centre, or take them off the lobby — which is better? Removed, with a badge
on FORGE in their place. The design record is in
[design/10](../10-ui-hud.md) ("The FORGE badge replaces the material chips"); this entry is the work.

**Why not the top centre.** The five counts are the elemental crafting materials, and the forge is
their only sink. The Forge and Loadout screens already show them (`MaterialBank`). On the lobby they
answered nothing: a total across tiers cannot say whether a recipe with `minTier` 2 is affordable,
or whether the staged kit leaves room for it, and the glyphs carry no label. Centring them would have
made them the screen's focal point, crowding the logo. The row's width also grew with the counts,
with nothing stopping it from running into the account card.

**What shipped.**

- `client/src/game/ui/LobbyResources.ts` is deleted, along with its tests and the
  `materials` field on `MainMenu.lobbyProfile`. The top-right corner is SETTINGS alone.
- `meta/forge.ts craftableNow(m)` counts the blueprints `craft()` would accept right now. It asks
  `craft()` itself rather than re-deriving its four gates (locked, loadout full, kind taken,
  unaffordable), so the badge cannot promise a craft the forge then refuses.
- `LobbyRoutes.setForgeReady(n)` draws a green pill on FORGE's top-right corner. The green is the
  one the forge paints a craftable card's status in. The pill is capped at `99+` and inset so it
  never pokes past the column. It is hidden at zero and for a non-finite or negative count.
  `gameAssembly.ts` feeds it `craftableNow(p.run.meta)` on every lobby show.

**Tests.** `forge.test.ts`: zero on an empty bank, a banked schematic adding exactly one, a staged
gun dropping the gun recipes, and a full loadout reaching zero. `MainMenu.test.ts`: no badge by
default, the corner holding only SETTINGS, the count and its corner position, hidden at 0 / −2 /
NaN, and the `99+` cap clearing on a later show at zero. The client typecheck is clean and the
client, engine and server suites are green.

Driven live in the dev server with 10 physical and 5 fire seeded into a fresh account: the corner
holds only SETTINGS, and FORGE reads 2. That is right, because a fresh account owns exactly two
recipes (`STARTER_BLUEPRINTS`: repeater and hammer), both physical ×3.
