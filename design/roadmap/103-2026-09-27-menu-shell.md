## One shell for every menu (2026-09-27, client + ui + test + i18n + docs, no engine change)

The owner, after the lobby pass (volume 102): the lobby looks good, but login, settings and the
other screens do not. They asked for an overall plan first, then one screen at a time, and chose
the lobby painting, dimmed, as the backdrop — for the in-run screens too. The design and its
reasons are in [design/10](../10-ui-hud.md#one-shell-for-every-menu-2026-09-27); this entry is the
work.

**The order, agreed up front and kept:** step 0 the shell, then Login, Settings, Loadout, Forge +
Store, Squad / Matchmaking / PvP preview, and the in-run Pause / Results. Each step was driven live
in the dev server at desktop size and at 844x390 before the next began.

**Step 0 — the shell.** New under `client/src/game/ui/`:

- `menuTheme.ts` — `MENU_COLORS`, `menuText(role)`, `MENU_BUTTONS` (primary, secondary, danger,
  pvp, chrome) and `MENU_BACKDROP_DIM`.
- `MenuSheet.ts` — the framed card and its title plate (`SHEET_TITLE_H` 58, `SHEET_PAD` 24),
  `placeHeading` for a section heading. Pure drawing, no press targets.
- `MenuShell.ts` — backdrop + top-left BACK chip + optional top-right chip + the sheet, with
  `layout(w, h, sheetW, sheetH)` scaling by `lobbyScale` capped by what fits. `mount` puts the
  backdrop at the screen view's child 0, which is what `viewportFit` skips.

`GameLoop` gained `menuScreens`, whose `animate` runs every frame outside a live sim, paused
included; `lobbyScreens` still update only outside a run.

**The screens.** Login (`loginSheet.ts`, new `FormField`, `TextInputOverlay` gained `anchor`,
`value` and `onBlur`); Settings (`settingsSheet.ts`, `Slider` split out of `widgets.ts` and
restyled); Loadout (`loadoutSheet.ts`, new shared `MaterialBank`, the SETTINGS chip moved from a
viewport-pinned position into the shell's corner, so `ScreenFlow.repositionSettingsButtonIfLoadout`
is gone); Forge (`forgeSheet.ts`) and Store; Squad (`partySheet.ts`, one row per seat),
Matchmaking (the crystal ring) and PvP preview; Pause and Results. Every screen that had its own
BACK button now uses the shell's chip; the results screen's MAIN MENU became that chip too.
Locale strings were added or reworded in all eight locales for the new headings, tabs and hints.

**Tests.** New: `MenuShell`, `FormField`, `Slider`, `MaterialBank`, `menuBackdrop`. Every screen's
test was rewritten against the sheet — flow order, full-width primaries, the chip firing the
screen's back callback, the sheet growing and shrinking with optional rows. Harness adjustments:
`labelFit`'s per-screen button census, `buttonCueConventions`' dismiss floor 10 → 8 (a real drop,
explained in the test), `gameUiSound` pressing the chip, `viewportFit` for the new layouts.

A last round, asked for after the screens were done, checked what the suite did not pin and added
eight tests: the results badge's row (present and closed up again), both the pause and results
sheets' tallest forms fitting 844x390, an offer that settles after the results screen was left
not re-laying it out, the pause hairline sitting between the groups, the login input opening ON
the field it edits (never asserted before, though it is the point of the login redesign), and the
folded password form refusing a submit. Each was checked by breaking the code it covers and
watching it fail. One planned test was dropped: the login "never laid out → centred prompt"
fallback cannot be reached under test, because an un-laid-out field still has a non-zero size; on
a real screen such a field is invisible and cannot be tapped.

**Verification.** Driven live at desktop sizes and at 844x390. Client suite 7,769 tests green
(328 files, up from 7,651 / 323); client coverage 98.33% lines / 94.61% branches; `tsc --noEmit`,
the file-length gate (no source file over 500 lines) and the doc-path check clean.
