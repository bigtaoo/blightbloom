The first ask quotes a diagnosis **this session had given** — that Blightbloom's art has no single
hand — and asks whether it can be fixed first. It was measured instead of argued, and the

## The measurement (and why the first attempt at it lied)

Measured over the **126 shipped sprites in `client/public/`**. The first pass ran over `art/`
and produced a falsely incoherent reading: that tree also holds `_raw` / `_original` / `_alt` /
`_master` pipeline intermediates, and an `environment/` folder of elemental bullets whose hues
*must* differ — the folder exists to differ. Only what the game actually loads is evidence.

| property | result |
|---|---|
| light direction | 87°, R = 0.70 over all 126 (biome 0.98, ui 0.81, skins 0.80, environment 0.75) |
| the one outlier | `weapons` at 58°, R = 0.58 — about 50° off the rest of the world |
| palette | 76/126 inside the 180–210° teal band, 27 inside the 0–30° warm band |
| value key | floor/biome 0.187 against actors ~0.50 |
| edges | outline strength 0.37 on actors, 0.05 on biome |

A unified direction exists and is being followed. The charge is not sustained, and the money it
would have taken was redirected.

## What the same count found instead

The **cast**, not the direction:

- 6 bosses → **1 rig** (`bodyRig: 'boss-core'` six times in `engine/content/bosses.ts`);
- ~10 enemy types → **3 rigs**, each a single `body.png`;
- 3 player skins → **one** 5-part rig, down to a byte-identical `socket_l.png`.

And the three enemy bundles shipped **byte-identical `animation.json`** (md5 `0d601f03…`), which
is where the thinness is actually visible: floor 1 room 1 is `basic` (critter-core), room 2 is
`floater`/`stalker` (floater-core), room 4 is `brute`/`ravager` (brute-core). A player who got to
room 4 watched a scuttling bug, a hovering drone and a heavy walk the same walk. 36.5% of the
Playgama sandbox's sessions never reached 60 seconds (`design/22` §4.1).

## Three gaits

Data only — no new art, no engine change, no `ENGINE_VERSION` bump, no determinism surface:

- **critter — "scurry".** Three patters per 0.6 s `move` cycle (was two); `idle` 2 s → 1.4 s with
  a scale breath so it never quite settles.
- **brute — "lumber".** One heavy step per cycle, and the only one of the three whose `move`
  touches `scaleX`: the widening at t=0.48 **is** the landing. `idle` 3 s, a 120 ms attack
  haul-back, a spawn that drops in from −14, a death that topples through 46°.
- **floater — "drift".** Lateral travel dominates vertical, so it never plants a foot — it must
  not, since `actorLift`'s `HOVER` already holds it 8.5 world px off the floor. Its `attack` is
  the one that gets shoved backwards, nose up, with nothing to brace against.

Every shared contract holds unchanged and is still asserted over all seven bundles: the six-clip
vocabulary, the 0.6 s-loop / 0.35 s-once cadence, the additive-overlay identity rule, `spawn` and
`death` as base-layer lifecycle clips, the t=0-keyframe rule, and the peak-translate bound against
each rig's drawn half-width.

## The test inverted, not deleted

`rigComposition.test.ts` asserted *"the three one-bone enemy bundles ship byte-identical
animations"*. Its reason was sound — only `critter-core` has a build script, so a one-sided edit
could drift silently — but it also enforced the sameness this pass removes. It now reads
*"…carry three deliberately different gaits"*: each bundle differs from the other two, **and** each
keeps the property that makes its gait its own, sampled through `sampleClip` rather than read off
the keyframes. Three named properties, each verified by making exactly its own mutation and
watching that one case fail alone (225 passed / 1 failed, three times):

| rig | pinned property | mutation that breaks it |
|---|---|---|
| critter | 3 footfalls per `move` cycle | copying another bundle's `move` in |
| brute | 1 footfall **and** a landing squash (peak `scaleX` − 1 > 0.05) | flattening `move`'s scale to 1 |
| floater | peak \|`translateX`\| > peak \|`translateY`\| | swapping the two channels |

`build-critter-project.mjs` told the next reader to copy any clip change into the other two
bundles; it now says the opposite, and `animations` joined `bindings` and `body.png` under that
script's existing **"the shipped bundle is ground truth"** rule — a re-run keeps whatever
`critter-core` ships and cannot revert the gait.

## Still open, deliberately

- **The weapons sprites' ~50° lighting offset.** The one real defect the measurement found.
  Fixing it is per-pixel relighting verifiable only by eye, and this pass had no way to check its
  own work.
- **A real enemy telegraph.** `attack` is a **recoil, not a windup** — `ClipLayers.attack()`'s own
  comment is "an attack just left this rig", and the 60 ms wind-back plays after the bullet is
  out. No telegraph exists anywhere in the sim. Adding one is a 7th clip across all seven bundles
  plus a sim-side wind-up state: `ENGINE_VERSION`, the golden-hash gate, a balance pass. A product
  decision, not a render one.
- **Six distinct boss rigs.** The right fix for the boss count and the wrong place to spend next:
  median runs per session is 1.11 and nobody is reaching floor 5.
