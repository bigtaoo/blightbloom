Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-09  ·  Blightbloom  ·  support@gamestao.com

This Privacy Policy explains what data Blightbloom collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies wherever you play the game — on our own site and on third-party game portals such as CrazyGames. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data protection law.

An account is never required to play Blightbloom, and your saved game stays in your own browser until you create one. As a guest, your settings and progress are stored locally and are never sent to us. Two things are sent even without an account, and both are described in full below: anonymous gameplay statistics (§2), which tell us which parts of the game get played and whether players come back, and error reports (§2) when something breaks. Neither carries your name, and neither is shared with anyone.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is Tao Wang, an independent sole trader (not an incorporated company) based in Germany, reachable at support@gamestao.com.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • No email address. Account registration asks for a username and password only.
  • No third-party analytics provider. The gameplay statistics in §2 are collected by our own server and go nowhere else. There is no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracking script anywhere in the game.
  • No advertising identifier and no cross-site tracking. We do not build a profile of you and do not track you across other sites or apps.
  • No sale or sharing of your data, for any purpose.
  • No free text. The events in §2 are a fixed list of names and numbers. Nothing you type is collected, and the game has no chat.
  • No payment data. See §5.

4. Local Storage in Your Browser

The game stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage rather than in cookies. There are four items, and you can clear them at any time through your browser's site-data controls:

  • daydayup.session.v1 — your session token, if you are signed in
  • daydayup.settings.v1 — your settings, such as volume and language
  • daydayup.meta.v1 — your local progress
  • daydayup.playerId.v1 — a random installation id, created by your browser. It identifies this installation of the game to itself: it is how a guest is recognised in multiplayer, and it is the id attached to the gameplay statistics in §2. Clearing it makes this browser a new installation as far as we are concerned.

These keys carry the project's original working name, which is why they do not match the game's title. We use no third-party tracking cookies.

On a game portal the game runs inside an embedded frame, and some browsers block storage for embedded content. If that happens, a guest's progress simply does not persist between sessions; nothing breaks, and a signed-in player is unaffected because their progress is held on our server.

5. Payments

Blightbloom currently sells nothing, and no payment processor is active. No purchase can be made, and we neither receive nor store any payment or billing data. If that changes, this policy will be updated before any payment feature becomes available, and the payment processor would hold your card and billing details directly — we would receive only the amount, the product, and a transaction identifier.

6. Advertising

On our own site, the game shows no ads at all.

On a game portal, ads are served by the portal, not by us. Blightbloom uses only player-initiated rewarded video: you are offered an optional ad in exchange for an in-game reward, and nothing plays unless you accept it. The portal's own privacy policy governs that ad and any data it involves. We receive no advertising identifier and no information about you from it — only whether the ad finished.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with:

  • The game portal you are playing on (for example CrazyGames) — it hosts the page, tells the game who you are if you are signed in there, and serves ads. Its own privacy policy applies to the portal page: crazygames.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of the game client
  • Our server hosting provider — running the game server. Where any provider processes data outside the EEA, it does so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Legal authorities — only where required by law

8. Data Retention

Account data and game state are kept for as long as your account exists, and are deleted when you ask us to delete the account. Server logs containing technical data are short-lived and kept only as long as needed for security and debugging. Because we take no payments, we hold no transaction records and are under no accounting-law obligation to retain them.

The gameplay statistics and error reports in §2 have their own fixed limits, and these are limits the software deletes on rather than intentions we hold:

  • Individual gameplay events: 90 days, then deleted.
  • The day-level record that an installation was active: 180 days, then deleted. That record is one installation id and one date, with no event detail, and it is what return rates are computed from. 180 days is what a full three-month return curve needs, and nothing is kept past it.
  • Error reports: 14 days, then deleted.
  • Day-level totals — how many installations played on a given day, how many runs were started — are kept without a time limit. These are aggregate counts only: no id of any kind is part of them, and no individual can be identified from them.

9. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access your data, correct inaccurate data, erase your data ("right to be forgotten"), restrict or object to processing, and receive your data in a portable form. To exercise any of these, email support@gamestao.com. We will respond within 30 days, free of charge.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For the two rows in §2 that do not need an account, what we can do depends on whether you have one, and the reason is worth stating plainly. If you are signed in, those records carry your account id, so they are found and deleted with the rest of your account data. If you play as a guest, they carry only the random installation id from §4 — which exists solely in your own browser, is not linked to your name, your email or anything else we hold, and which we therefore have no way to look up on your behalf. Clearing that key (or your site data) is the remedy that actually works, and it is one only you can apply. We would rather say this than imply we can identify a guest.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Because an account is optional and no email address is ever collected, a child can play the game without providing any personal data to us at all. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Security

All traffic to our site and game server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes and are compared in constant time, so we cannot read your password. The account database is backed up daily. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we apply measures appropriate to the limited data we hold.
